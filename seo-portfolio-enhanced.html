03 / SEO Skills

Six pillars.
One complete strategy.

📄
On-Page SEO
Content & Structure Optimisation

Optimising every element that lives on the webpage — from title tags and meta descriptions to heading hierarchy, keyword placement, and internal linking.

  • Title tag & meta description writing with target keywords
  • H1–H3 heading structure with keyword hierarchy
  • Keyword density & semantic keyword (LSI) placement
  • Internal linking strategy across all pages
  • Image alt text optimisation with geo + keyword combos
  • URL structure: clean, descriptive, keyword-rich slugs
  • Content gap analysis vs. competitor pages
🔗
Off-Page SEO
Authority & Link Building

Building the website's credibility through quality backlinks, citations, and brand mentions — all signals Google uses to rank pages higher.

  • Local citation building: JustDial, Sulekha, IndiaMart
  • Google Maps & Zomato listing optimisation
  • Food blogger outreach for backlink mentions
  • Guest posting on Surat lifestyle/food blogs
  • Social signals: Instagram, Facebook business pages
  • Review management strategy (Google, Zomato)
  • Competitor backlink gap analysis
⚙️
Technical SEO
Site Health & Crawlability

Ensuring search engines can properly crawl, index, and render the website — covering speed, mobile-friendliness, structured data, and architecture.

  • Schema.org markup: LocalBusiness, Menu, FAQPage
  • XML sitemap creation & submission to GSC
  • Robots.txt configuration for proper crawling
  • Core Web Vitals audit (LCP, CLS, INP)
  • Mobile responsiveness testing & fixing
  • HTTPS security & canonical tag implementation
  • Page speed optimisation (image compression, lazy load)
📍
Local SEO
Geo-Targeted Visibility

Dominating local search results so that customers in Surat find Maymuna's Cafe when searching for cafes, coffee, or bakeries nearby.

  • Google Business Profile setup & full optimisation
  • NAP consistency: Name, Address, Phone across all platforms
  • Geo-targeted keywords: "cafe in Pal Road Surat", etc.
  • Local landing page with embedded Google Maps
  • Review generation campaign for star rating boost
  • GBP Posts: weekly offers, photos, announcements
  • "Near me" keyword targeting & optimisation
🤖
AEO — Answer Engine Optimisation
Featured Snippets & Voice Search

Optimising content to appear in Google's Answer Boxes, People Also Ask sections, and voice search results — capturing zero-click traffic.

  • FAQ schema implementation on menu & about pages
  • Question-based keyword targeting ("what cafe is open in Surat?")
  • Structured Q&A content blocks on key pages
  • Concise, definition-first paragraph writing (40–60 words)
  • Voice search phrase optimisation (conversational queries)
  • People Also Ask (PAA) content expansion
  • HowTo schema for coffee brewing content
✨
GEO — Generative Engine Optimisation
AI Search Visibility

Optimising content to be cited and recommended by AI tools like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Perplexity when users ask about cafes in Surat.

  • Entity-based SEO: clear brand entity signals across web
  • Authoritative "About" content with facts, history, unique claims
  • Structured data to make content machine-readable
  • High E-E-A-T signals (Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trust)
  • Consistent NAP + brand mentions across platforms
  • Wikipedia-style factual writing for AI ingestion
  • Monitoring AI citation tools (Perplexity, Gemini, ChatGPT)

Google PageSpeed Insights
Google · Core Web Vitals
PageSpeed Insights measures how fast a website loads on mobile and desktop — a direct Google ranking factor. I use it to diagnose and fix speed issues.
  • LCP (Largest Contentful Paint): should be under 2.5s
  • CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift): should be under 0.1
  • INP (Interaction to Next Paint): should be under 200ms
  • Fix: compress images, remove unused CSS/JS, enable caching
Sample Report — Maymuna's Cafe Mobile ↓
URL: memunagandhi.github.io/cafe-website/ | Device: Mobile
Performance: 62/100 ⚠️ | LCP: 4.1s ✕ (fix: compress hero image) | CLS: 0.08 ✓ | INP: 180ms ✓ | Opportunity: "Reduce image sizes — saves 2.1s" | Fix: Convert hero to WebP format, add loading="lazy"
📚 How to use for real work: Test every page at pagespeed.web.dev. Score 90+ on mobile = good. The biggest fix is always images — convert JPG/PNG to WebP format and compress. This alone can improve score by 20+ points.
